# Jobshift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31.10.2026

Jobshift ("we", "us") is an app that connects students with short-term shifts offered by employers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. The app is operated by Bodycalc.

**Contact:** bodycalc@help.gmail.com 

## Who can use Jobshift

Jobshift is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## Information we collect

**From students**
- Name, email address, phone number, city and college
- A photo of your student ID, used only to verify that you are a student
- Your UPI ID, so we can pay you for completed shifts
- Your activity in the app: shifts you apply to, accepted shifts, check-in and check-out times, hours worked, payment status and payment reference (UTR)
- Reports or messages you send us through the app

**From employers**
- Email address, company name, contact person, phone number and business address
- GSTIN (optional) and a photo or document of your business proof, used to verify your business
- Shifts you post and your decisions about applicants

**Automatically**
- Our server logs basic technical data such as IP address, request time and device type, for security and to fix problems.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or camera or gallery content except for the single photo you choose to upload.

## How we use your information

- To create and manage your account
- To verify students and employers before they can use the service
- To show shifts, process applications and record attendance
- To calculate and make payments to students for completed shifts
- To handle reports, disputes and support requests
- To keep the service secure and prevent misuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads or use advertising or analytics trackers.

## What other people can see

- **Employers** can see your name, college and city when you apply to their shift. They see your phone number only after they accept you. They never see your ID photo or UPI ID.
- **Students** never see other students' information. Students see only an employer's company name on a shift.
- **We** (the operator) can see all information in the app, because we verify accounts, run payments and handle disputes.

## Sharing with third parties

We do not share your information with third parties for their own purposes. Limited technical services are involved in running the app:
- **Our hosting:** data is stored on a server we operate. It is reached over the internet through Tailscale, which relays encrypted traffic.
- **WhatsApp:** if you tap "Contact us on WhatsApp", WhatsApp opens and your messages are handled under WhatsApp's own policy.
- **Google Play:** distributes the app and may collect its own data under Google's policy.
- **Legal reasons:** we may disclose information if required by law or to protect people's safety.

## Payments

Payments to students are made by us outside the app, using the UPI ID you provide. We keep records of payments (amount, date, reference number) for accounting and dispute handling.

## How long we keep your data

- Account and activity data: for as long as your account is active.
- ID photos and business proofs: until your account is deleted, or [NUMBER] months after your last activity, whichever is earlier.
- Payment and attendance records: up to [NUMBER] years, where we need them for accounting or legal reasons.

## Security

Data travels between the app and our server over HTTPS. Access to the admin system is limited to us, and files such as ID photos are not publicly accessible. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:
- Show you the data we hold about you
- Correct wrong information
- Delete your account and your personal data

To make a request, email bodycalc.help@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp from the app, with the email address on your account. We will respond within [30] days. We may keep limited information where the law requires it, such as payment records.

## Account deletion

To delete your account, send a request with the subject "Delete my account" to bodycalc.help@gmail.com, or use "Report a problem" in the app (Profile → Report a problem). We will delete your account and personal data, except records we must keep, within [30] days.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

## Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your data, contact us first at bodycalc.help@gmail.com. We will try to resolve it promptly.
