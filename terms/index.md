## Part B: Extra terms for students

### 13. Applying and booking
Applying does not guarantee a place. The employer chooses who to accept. When an employer accepts you, you are booked for that shift.

### 14. Your relationship with the employer
When you work a shift, you work for the employer, not for Jobshift. Follow the employer's reasonable instructions, the dress code, and the safety rules at the venue.

### 15. Attendance and cancellation
- Arrive on time. If you can't attend, cancel in the app as early as you can. Repeated no-shows or last-minute cancellations may lead to your account being suspended.
- Check in only when you are at the venue, using the code the employer gives you in person. Check out the same way when you finish.
- Check-in and check-out records may be used to settle questions about hours and pay.

### 16. Payment
- The employer agrees the pay for each shift and is responsible for paying you directly, **at the end of the shift or as agreed in the shift details**. Jobshift does not pay you and does not hold your money.
- Ask for payment before you leave the venue. Keep your UPI receipt or other proof.
- If you are not paid, tell us straight away using **Report a problem > I was not paid**, and give us the details. We will contact the employer and may suspend them, but we cannot promise that you will be paid.
- You may confirm in the app that you received payment.
- You are responsible for any tax you owe on what you earn.

### 17. Your information
You agree that the employer may see your name, college and city when you apply, and your phone number once they accept you. They never see your ID photo or UPI ID.

### 18. Safety
Do not go to a shift that feels unsafe. Leave if you feel at risk, and tell us. Do not accept any task that is illegal or very different from the shift description.

### 19. Your business
You confirm that you are authorised to act for the business you register, that the details and documents you give are true, and that you will keep them up to date. We may ask for more documents at any time.

### 20. Posting shifts
- Every shift is reviewed before it is shown, and we may reject or edit a listing. Reviewing a shift does not mean we approve of it or guarantee that it is lawful or safe.
- A shift must be accurate: real work, the real date, time, place, pay, dress code and number of workers needed.
- Shifts must be lawful. You must follow all laws that apply to you as an employer, including laws on wages, working hours, safety, and non-discrimination.
- You are responsible for the safety of workers at your venue.

### 21. Accepting workers
You choose which applicants to accept, up to the number of workers you listed. Once you accept someone, you are expected to honour the booking. Cancel only if you have to, and as early as you can.

### 22. Paying workers
- You are the employer for the shift and **you are responsible for paying every worker the agreed pay in full, directly to the worker, at the end of the shift** , by UPI or other electronic payment where possible.
- Jobshift does not collect, hold or guarantee payments to workers.
- You must keep a record of each payment, and in the app you may be asked to enter the payment reference (UTR) for each worker.
- You must follow the law on minimum wages and overtime. The pay you list must not be below what the law requires for the work.
- If you fail to pay a worker, we may suspend or permanently remove your account and shifts, and we may share the shift and attendance record with the worker so they can claim what they are owed.

### 23. Check-in codes
We give you a code for each shift. Give it to workers in person, only when they are at the venue. Do not post it in a chat group or share it in advance.

### 24. Fees
Jobshift is free during the pilot. We may start charging fees later, for example a percentage of the pay of the workers you hire, or a flat fee per worker. We will tell you in advance, and the fee will apply only to shifts posted after the notice. Any fee will be invoiced separately and is never taken from a worker's pay.

### 25. Hiring outside Jobshift
You agree not to use Jobshift only to find workers and then avoid the fees that apply to hires made through it.

### 26. Worker information
You may use a worker's name, college, city and phone number only to run the shift they were booked for. You must not store them for other purposes, share them, or contact workers for anything unrelated to the shift.

### 27. Indemnity
You agree to cover the costs, losses and claims we suffer because of your breach of these terms, your shifts, or your failure to pay workers, to the extent the law allows.
